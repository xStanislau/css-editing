# Product brief

## Product hypothesis
Adults who enjoy "solve-it-yourself" mysteries (murder-mystery boxes, escape-room fans, puzzle-podcast listeners)
will pay a small one-off price for a polished, atmospheric browser case they can finish in one sitting
without an account, an app install or a group booking. Once one case is proven, an engine that turns typed case
data into a playable case should let us release more packs at low marginal cost, with no custom client work.

## Audience (assumed, not yet validated)
- Solo players or couples, playing on phone or laptop in the evening.
- Enjoy fair-play puzzles: every answer can be deduced from what's given.
- Prefer intrigue and atmosphere over gore. The tone is non-graphic.

## What a case must provide
- One clear question, and one logically supported answer reachable only from in-game evidence.
- At least one contradiction the player must notice.
- Hints that get progressively more helpful. No timers, no outside knowledge, no guesswork.
- It must work on phones and desktops, with keyboard use and readable text.

## Current scope (Checkpoints 0–1)
One location (Hotel Vesper), 3 suspects, 6 evidence items, 3 hints, a final deduction with an explanation, restart,
and local save. Placeholder art only.

## Explicit exclusions for now
Backend, accounts, analytics, payments/checkout, runtime AI, paid asset generation, public deployment,
additional cases, and a general-purpose content platform/CMS.
